About me

M|M engineer 0.99

I'm a Computer Engineering student from Tehran who got hooked on computer vision. I love problems where a camera feed turns into useful information: a license plate read correctly, a vehicle tracked along a highway, a model that keeps working when the light changes.

My work covers the whole pipeline: collecting and annotating data, training deep learning models, and making them fast enough for real-time use. I rely on transfer learning to get strong results from small datasets, and I enjoy teams that test ideas quickly and learn from every result.

Right now I'm working remotely in computer vision and looking for a place to grow as an engineer on real products.
