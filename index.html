About me

I'm a Computer Engineering student in Tehran who got hooked on computer vision. I like problems where a camera feed turns into useful information: a license plate read correctly, a vehicle tracked across a highway, a model that still works when the lighting changes.

My work covers the full pipeline: collecting and annotating data, choosing and training deep learning models, and making them run fast enough for real-time use. I rely on transfer learning to get strong results from small datasets, and I enjoy working in a team where ideas get tested quickly.

I'm currently working remotely in computer vision and looking for opportunities to grow as an engineer on real products.
